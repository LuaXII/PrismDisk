Notes on this installer:

- PrismDisk reads NTFS volumes directly through the Master File Table (MFT) to
  analyze disk usage. It does not modify files during a scan.
- "Move to Recycle Bin" can be undone from the Recycle Bin. "Delete
  permanently" and the Smart Cleanup feature remove files for good; PrismDisk
  always asks before deleting anything.
- Installing PrismDisk creates shortcuts in your Start menu and, optionally, on
  your desktop. Uninstalling removes the program folder and those shortcuts;
  it never touches your data.
